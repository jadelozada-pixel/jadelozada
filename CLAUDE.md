# Instructions for Claude

This repository is a journalist's personal website. GitHub Pages publishes it from the main branch.

- Commit every change straight to main and push it, so the site updates. Don't open pull requests or leave work on other branches.
- Use my words exactly as I give them: my About text, headlines, outlet names and dates. Don't rewrite, shorten, summarize or add to them. If something is missing, leave a clearly marked spot and ask me.
- Never add facts about me that I didn't give you.
- Keep the site simple: one index.html with its styles inside it, and photos in an images folder. No build tools or frameworks.
- When you finish, tell me in plain words what changed, and that the live site can take a minute or two to update.

## Site style

Keep the look of a classic newspaper or magazine page, like The New York Times or The Atlantic: serif type and plenty of space. Future changes should keep this style.

- Fonts: Libre Caslon Text for my name, Source Serif 4 for everything else, loaded from Google Fonts with Georgia as the fallback.
- Layout: one narrow centered column (about 640px wide), with generous space between sections.
- Header: my name centered and large, my beat in italics below it, between a double rule above and a thin rule below.
- Section labels (About, Clips, Contact): small uppercase letters with wide spacing, under a thin gray line.
- Colors: near-black text on white, muted gray for secondary text, and a quiet blue for links. Keep the dark-mode version that matches.
- No photos, icons or decoration I haven't asked for.
