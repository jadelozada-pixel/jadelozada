About

I cover economic policy at The Washington Sun.

I previously interned on the Boston Globe’s metro desk, the investigations team at WGBH, the Associated Press’s United Nations bureau and Civil.ge, an independent news site in Tbilisi, Georgia.

I received my degree in social studies and Slavic languages and literature at Harvard College, where I served as magazine editor at large for The Harvard Crimson.
